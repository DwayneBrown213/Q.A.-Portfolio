## 📝 Description
Requirements specify the map should show **remaining cooking and delivery time** from each restaurant to the pick-up point. The app **does not display remaining cooking time**.

### **Precondition:**  
- Order placed; tracking in progress.
 
### **Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Place an order.  
2. Open **Order Tracking**.  
3. Look for remaining cooking time.


### **Expected Result:**  
Display **remaining cooking time** (and delivery time) for each restaurant to the pick-up point.
### **Actual Result:**  
**No remaining cooking time** is displayed anywhere on the screen.

**OS:** *Windows 10 Home 22H2*<br>
