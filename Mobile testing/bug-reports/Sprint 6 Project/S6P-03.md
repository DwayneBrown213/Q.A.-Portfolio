## 📝 Description
On the Order Confirmation screen, the total reflects only the dishes’ prices.

### **Precondition:**  
- Pick-up point selected; multiple dishes in cart.  
- User is on **Order Confirmation** screen.
 
### **Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Add several dishes to the order.  
2. Proceed to **Order Confirmation**.  
3. Compare displayed total vs. expected (dishes + delivery).

### **Expected Result:**  
Total amount contains the price of all prepared dishes and delivery.
### **Actual Result:**  
Total shows **dishes only**; **Price of delivery** is missing.

**OS:** *Windows 10 Home 22H2*<br>
