## 📝 Description
On the “Select pick-up location” screen, the app lists/plots pick-up points with no discernible ordering. QA notes: “Pick-up points are not in any order; they are all listed randomly.”

### **Precondition:**  
- App launched with no active order.
 
### **Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Open the Urban Lunch web app.  
2. Navigate to the default “Select pick-up location” screen.  
3. Observe the list/markers of pick-up points.



### **Expected Result:**  
The map shows the order of pick-up points
### **Actual Result:**  
Pick-up points appear **unordered/random**.
