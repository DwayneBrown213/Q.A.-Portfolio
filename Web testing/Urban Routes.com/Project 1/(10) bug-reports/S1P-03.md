## 📝 Description

### **Precondition:**  
 Map screen is open; “From”(or “To”) field is empty.
 
### **Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Enter the application’s web address in the browser.
2. Click the “From” (or “To”) field.
3. Enter any valid address.

### **Expected Result:**  
The map is zoomed in at the address pin. The view matches the design description.
### **Actual Result:**  
Addresses enterin into the app do not trigger zoom or directions; the view does not match the design.
The map only zooms-in to the two gray addresses that appear after the first click on “From/To” fields. After both gray addresses are filled, the map zooms with directions. No other addresses in the app trigger zoom or directions; the view does not match the design.

**OS:** *Windows 10 Home 22H2*<br>
