## 📝 Description

### **Precondition:**  
 In the web browser the "About Customer” form is displayed and blank.
 
### **Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Enter the application’s web address in the browser.
2. Accept cookies.
3. Click the order button.
4. Enter more than 15 Latin letters in the Last name field.
5. Click the “Next” button at the bottom of the screen.


### **Expected Result:**  
The error message highlighted in red *"Enter a valid last name"* should be displayed.
### **Actual Result:**  
More than 15 Latin letters ***are*** accepted in the Last name field

**OS:** *Windows 10 Home 22H2*<br>

- *Opera One Version: 116.0 (Chromium version:131.0)*
