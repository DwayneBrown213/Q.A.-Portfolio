## 📝 Description

### **Precondition:**  
 In the web browser the "About Customer” form is displayed and blank.
 
### **Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Enter the application’s web address in the browser.
2. Accept cookies.
3. Click the order button.
4. Enter a + sign with a valid amount of 9 numbers in the Phone field, a total of 10 characters.
5. Click the “Next” button at the bottom of the screen.

### **Expected Result:**  
 A + sign with a valid amount of numbers are *accepted* in the Phone field

### **Actual Result:**  
10 characters are *not accepted* in the Phone field.
