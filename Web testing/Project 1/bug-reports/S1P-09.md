## 📝 Description

### **Precondition:**  
 Map screen is open; Street View picture is visible.
 
### **Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Zoom in on map.
2. Drag the Street View mode icon and place it on any street.
3. Scroll over the picture.

   
### **Expected Result:**  
The picture scrolls 360 degrees.

### **Actual Result:**  
Scrolling over the picture, changes the zoom level. It does not rotate the picture in any angle or degrees. Click and dragging the picture from one side to another rotates the picture 360 degrees.

**OS:** *Windows 10 Home 22H2*<br>
**Browsers:** 
- *Chrome Version 133.0*<br>
